# Where Telegram gifts trade

Telegram gifts trade on seven main venues. In the 30 days to 13 November 2026 they handled 39.9M TON across 1.86M sales, and MRKT and Portals carried almost four fifths of that volume.

| Marketplace | Type | Share of 30 day volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MRKT | Telegram Mini App, TON balance | 44.1% |
| Portals | Telegram Mini App, TON balance | 34.2% |
| Telegram | Resale market inside the app, Stars or TON | 12.4% |
| Tonnel | Telegram Mini App | 4.7% |
| Getgems | NFT marketplace on TON, connected wallet | 2.9% |
| Fragment | Telegram's platform for TON collectibles | 1.2% |
| Market App | Telegram Mini App | 0.5% |

## How they differ

- MRKT has the most volume and the most sales.
- Portals has fewer sales but a larger typical sale, and the most live listings.
- Telegram's own market sells many gifts, mostly cheaper ones.
- Getgems, Fragment and Market App see far fewer sales, yet some of the month's largest sales happened there.

## Buying and selling tips

- No venue is always cheapest. The cheapest listing of a gift moves between venues during the day.
- Price against completed sales of the same model, not the collection floor.
- Check how many of your gift are listed on each venue. Fewer competing listings usually means a faster sale.
- Fees differ and change. Check them on the venue before you trade.
