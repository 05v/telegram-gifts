# Telegram gift rarity and serial numbers

Two upgraded gifts from the same collection can differ in price a hundred times over. The difference is rarity, which works on two levels: how many of the gift exist at all, and which traits each one rolled.

## The traits

- **Model**: the artwork. Usually sets the price level inside a collection.
- **Backdrop**: the coloured background. Black and Onyx Black are popular enough to carry their own floor.
- **Symbol**: the small icon repeated across the backdrop.
- **Serial number**: #1 upward. Low numbers and patterns sell at a premium.

Each trait shows a percentage, the share of the collection with that trait. Lower is rarer. Odds multiply: a 1% model on a 1% backdrop is about one in 10,000. Telegram groups models into Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary tiers.

## Numbers buyers look for

- One, two and three digit numbers, such as #7, #42 or #350.
- Repeated digits, such as #777 or #1111.
- Sequences, palindromes and round hundreds or thousands.

Two of the biggest sales on record as of November 2026 were low numbers: Plush Pepe #2 (Hothead) at 158,814 TON and Resistance Dog #1 (Peacekeeper) at 64,750 TON. Plush Pepe #88 (Yellow Hug) sold for 55,000 TON.

## Supply changes

Gifts used in Telegram's crafting feature are burned and leave the collection for good, so rarity can shift over time. As of November 2026, 12.07M upgraded gifts were indexed across 7,940 models.
