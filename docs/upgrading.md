# Upgrading Telegram gifts

Upgrading turns a regular Telegram gift into a unique collectible. You pay a fee in Stars and Telegram gives the gift a serial number and three random traits: a model, a backdrop and a symbol.

## Steps

1. Open the gift on your profile or in the chat where you received it.
2. Tap the upgrade option. Telegram previews the models, backdrops and symbols it can roll.
3. Choose whether to keep the original details (sender, recipient, message). Removing them costs extra.
4. Pay the fee in Stars. The cost differs from gift to gift.

A sender can prepay the upgrade, so the recipient upgrades for free.

## What to know first

- The roll is random and permanent. No rerolls, no undo.
- An upgraded gift can no longer be converted into Stars, but it can be transferred or sold.
- Most upgrades land on common traits and sell near the collection floor. A few land on rare ones and sell for many times more.
- As of November 2026 there were 125 upgradable collections with 7,940 models between them.

## Is it worth it

Compare the fee with what upgraded gifts of that collection actually sell for. Check the cheapest model floor on the collection page, for example [Plush Pepe](https://salestracker.tg/gifts/plushpepe) or [Myth Mask](https://salestracker.tg/gifts/mythmask), then recent completed sales on [Sales](https://salestracker.tg/sales). New rolls show up on [New upgrades](https://salestracker.tg/upgrades) shortly after they happen.
