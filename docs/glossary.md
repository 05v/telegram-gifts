# Telegram gift glossary

Plain definitions of the terms you meet when buying, selling or upgrading Telegram gifts.

## Gifts

- **Telegram gift**: an animated item bought with Stars and sent to a user or channel. It shows on the recipient's profile.
- **Stars**: Telegram's in app currency. Gifts and upgrade fees are paid in Stars.
- **Limited gift**: a gift issued in a fixed number. When it sells out, no new ones can be bought.
- **Upgrade**: paying Stars to turn a gift into a unique collectible. Permanent.
- **Upgraded gift, collectible**: a gift after its upgrade, with a serial number, model, backdrop and symbol.
- **Gift NFT**: an upgraded gift moved to the TON blockchain and held in a TON wallet.
- **Collection**: all gifts of one kind, such as Plush Pepe. There were 125 upgraded collections in November 2026.

## Traits and rarity

- **Model**: the artwork of an upgraded gift. A collection can have well over a hundred; Desk Calendar has 157.
- **Backdrop**: the coloured background behind the model.
- **Symbol**: the small icon repeated across the backdrop.
- **Rarity percentage**: the share of a collection's upgraded gifts with a trait. 1% means about one in a hundred.
- **Rarity tier**: Telegram's grouping of models into Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary.
- **Serial number**: the gift's place in its collection, #1 upward.
- **Monochrome**: a gift whose model colours match its backdrop.
- **Crafted model**: a model obtained through Telegram's crafting feature instead of an upgrade.
- **Burned**: a gift used up in crafting. It leaves the supply for good.
- **Supply**: how many gifts of a collection exist as collectibles. Plush Pepe had 2,825 in November 2026.

## Prices

- **Floor**: the cheapest listing right now, for a collection, a model or a backdrop.
- **Model floor**: the cheapest listing of one model. The fair comparison for a specific gift.
- **Last sale**: the most recent completed sale. Current, but one trade.
- **Median sale**: the middle price of completed sales over a period.
- **Average sale**: total volume divided by the number of sales. Pulled up by a few big trades.
- **Volume**: total TON paid in completed sales over a period.
- **Market cap**: floor times supply. All collections together were about 130.2M TON on 13 November 2026.
- **Deal**: a listing priced under what similar gifts usually sell for.

## Trading

- **TON, Toncoin**: the coin of The Open Network. Gifts, usernames and numbers are priced in it.
- **GRAM**: the name the Sales Tracker app uses for TON.
- **Marketplace, venue**: where gifts are listed and sold: Portals, MRKT, Tonnel, Getgems, Fragment, Market App or Telegram's own market.
- **Mini App**: an app that runs inside Telegram.
- **Listing**: a gift put up for sale at a fixed price.
- **Offer**: a bid a buyer makes on a gift, which the owner can accept.
- **Buyer fee**: a fee some venues add on top of the listed price.
- **Portfolio**: everything held by one profile or wallet, valued at today's prices.

## Usernames and numbers

- **Collectible username**: a Telegram @username held as a token on TON and traded on Fragment.
- **+888 number**: an anonymous number that exists only on Telegram and signs in without a SIM card.
- **Fragment**: Telegram's platform for TON collectibles at fragment.com.
- **Auction**: a sale where bids rise until a deadline.
