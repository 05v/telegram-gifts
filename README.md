# Telegram Gifts: a plain guide to prices, rarity and trading

Telegram gifts are collectible items you buy with Telegram Stars and show on your profile. Once upgraded, each gift becomes a unique collectible on the TON blockchain with its own model, backdrop, symbol and serial number, and it can be bought and sold like any other NFT.

This repository is a short, readable guide to how that market works. For live numbers (floors, last sales, market caps and every sale as it happens), use [Sales Tracker](https://salestracker.tg), a free tracker for Telegram gifts, collectible usernames and anonymous +888 numbers. It runs on the web, as a Telegram Mini App and as the inline bot [@SalesBot](https://t.me/SalesBot).

## What Telegram gifts are

A Telegram gift is an animated item bought with Stars, Telegram's in app currency, and sent to a user or a channel. It appears on the recipient's profile. Some gifts are limited: Telegram issues a fixed number, and once they sell out no new ones can be bought.

Many gifts can be **upgraded**. An upgraded gift is unique, has a public page at `t.me/nft/GiftName-Number`, can be moved to a TON wallet as an NFT, and can be sold on marketplaces inside and outside Telegram.

More: [What are Telegram gifts?](https://salestracker.tg/guides/what-are-telegram-gifts) and [Telegram gift NFTs explained](https://salestracker.tg/guides/telegram-gift-nft).

## Upgrading a gift into a collectible

You pay a fee in Stars, and Telegram gives the gift a serial number plus three random traits. The roll is permanent: you cannot pick or reroll a trait, and an upgraded gift can no longer be converted back into Stars. A sender can prepay the upgrade, in which case the recipient upgrades for free.

As of November 2026 there were 125 upgradable collections and 7,940 models between them. Some collections are huge in variety: Desk Calendar has 157 models, Jingle Bells 151 and Myth Mask 134.

Before paying the fee, compare it with what the cheapest upgraded gift of that collection actually sells for. If the cheapest one sells for less than your fee plus the gift, the odds favour not upgrading.

Full walkthrough: [How upgrading Telegram gifts works](https://salestracker.tg/guides/upgrading-telegram-gifts). Short version here: [docs/upgrading.md](docs/upgrading.md).

## Rarity: model, backdrop, symbol and number

Every upgraded gift carries four things:

| Part | What it is | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | The artwork | Usually sets the price level inside a collection |
| Backdrop | The coloured background | Adjusts the price; Black and Onyx Black are popular |
| Symbol | A small icon repeated across the backdrop | Adds a smaller premium |
| Serial number | The gift's place in the collection, #1 upward | Low numbers and patterns like #1111 sell higher |

Each trait shows a percentage: the share of that collection's upgraded gifts with the same trait. Lower is rarer. Traits roll independently, so the odds multiply: a 1% model on a 1% backdrop comes out about once in 10,000 upgrades. Telegram also groups models into tiers: Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary. A gift whose model colours match its backdrop is called **monochrome**.

Some models are **crafted**, coming from Telegram's crafting feature instead of a plain upgrade. Gifts used up in crafting are **burned** and leave the supply for good.

You can search every gift by trait or by number in the [Finder](https://salestracker.tg/finder), and browse [every backdrop](https://salestracker.tg/backdrops). More: [Telegram gift rarity and serial numbers](https://salestracker.tg/guides/telegram-gift-rarity) and [docs/rarity.md](docs/rarity.md).

## Floor price vs last sale vs median

These three numbers answer different questions, and mixing them up is the most common pricing mistake.

| Measure | What it is | Good for | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Floor | The cheapest listing right now | Cheapest way into a collection | A common model; says little about a rare one |
| Model floor | The cheapest listing of one model | Pricing a specific gift | Thin when few are listed |
| Last sale | The most recent completed sale | Current direction | One trade, can be an outlier |
| Median sale | Middle price of completed sales over a period | Steadiest view of what things sell for | Lags fast moves |

To price one gift: start from its model floor, check recent completed sales of the same model, then adjust for backdrop, symbol and number. Guides: [How to tell what a Telegram gift is worth](https://salestracker.tg/guides/telegram-gift-prices) and [Telegram gift floor price](https://salestracker.tg/guides/telegram-gift-floor-price).

## Collectible usernames and +888 numbers

Fragment also sells two other kinds of Telegram collectible:

- **Collectible usernames**: a Telegram @username held as a token on TON. Whoever holds it can put it on an account, channel or group, or sell it. Four letter names exist only as collectibles.
- **Anonymous +888 numbers**: phone numbers that exist only on Telegram. You can sign up and log in with one, with no SIM card.

As of 13 November 2026, Sales Tracker had recorded 655,497 username sales worth 113.5M TON and 366,449 +888 number sales worth 135.4M TON. Per sale, both are far larger than a typical gift sale.

## Glossary

Short definitions. The longer list is in [docs/glossary.md](docs/glossary.md).

- **Stars**: Telegram's in app currency, used to buy gifts and pay upgrade fees.
- **Upgrade**: paying Stars to turn a gift into a unique collectible. Permanent.
- **Model, backdrop, symbol**: the three random traits of an upgraded gift.
- **Floor**: the cheapest listing right now.
- **Median sale**: the middle price of completed sales over a period.
- **Volume**: total TON paid in completed sales over a period.
- **Market cap**: floor times supply.
- **Monochrome**: model colours match the backdrop.
- **Burned**: used up in crafting and gone from the supply.
- **TON**: the coin of The Open Network, the currency gifts trade in. The Sales Tracker app shows it as GRAM.

## FAQ

**What is the most expensive Telegram gift ever sold?**
Plush Pepe #2 (Hothead), for 158,814 TON on 8 January 2026, the highest sale on record as of November 2026.

**Which Telegram gift marketplace is the biggest?**
MRKT, with 44.1% of gift volume in the 30 days to 13 November 2026, then Portals with 34.2% and Telegram's own market with 12.4%.

**Do I need a TON wallet to buy Telegram gifts?**
Not for the Mini App marketplaces, where you deposit TON into a balance. Getgems works with a connected TON wallet, and Telegram's own market also accepts Stars.

**Can I choose the model when upgrading?**
No. Model, backdrop and symbol are random, and the result cannot be redone.

**What does the percentage on a gift mean?**
The share of that collection's upgraded gifts with the same trait. A 0.5% model appears on about one gift in two hundred.

**How do I check what my gifts are worth?**
Look up your profile or wallet in Sales Tracker's portfolio view. See [How to check the value of your Telegram gifts](https://salestracker.tg/guides/telegram-gift-portfolio-value).

**Is Fragment the same as Telegram's market?**
No. Fragment is Telegram's platform for TON collectibles, including usernames and +888 numbers. Telegram's market is the resale feature inside the Telegram app.
